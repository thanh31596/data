DATA4500: Marketing and Social Media Analytics

Learn marketing frameworks, social media analytics, generative AI for marketing, and predictive modelling — from STP and the 4Ps through engagement analysis, CLV, and churn.

Welcome to DATA4500! This week introduces the core marketing frameworks (3Cs, STP, 4Ps), how promotional channels have evolved into digital and social experiences, and how the consumer journey maps onto the conversion funnel.
